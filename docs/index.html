Use cases

  • Header menus to staging

    A site built locally — pages, a header menu with submenus and its theme location — repeated on staging without rebuilding the menu by hand.

  • Theme look to staging

    A change to a theme's look made locally — the last CSS rule, the logo, one widget, or a block theme's palette — taken to staging without touching what staging has of its own.

  • Site header to staging

    A block theme's header redone in the Site Editor locally — a new layout, its navigation, an image — and a page template, taken to staging in place of the ones staging has, without a Header 2.

  • Galleries to staging

    Pages built with Image, Gallery and Cover blocks, moved to a site where the media get other IDs — and still editable as they were.

  • Internal links to staging

    Pages that link to each other, to products and to categories, built locally and moved to staging — with every link pointing to staging, not back to the local site.

  • Product categories to staging

    A store's products moved with their categories as they are — descriptions, parent categories and thumbnails — not just their names.

  • Second delivery to staging

    Delivering new pages to a staging site that already has the media from the first delivery — without duplicating the images and without links to the local site.

  • Subsite to subsite

    On a network, a site built in one subsite moved to another — the live one, or a fresh copy — with its media, links and settings pointing to the right site.

  • Client demo in Playground

    A new page shown to a client in WordPress Playground: one link that opens right on that page, with the site's plugins as they are.

  • Playground in your language

    A demo of an English site shown in Portuguese in WordPress Playground: the dashboard, the theme and the plugins translated, from one link.

  • Custom post types in Playground

    A site whose post types live in a must-use plugin, shown in WordPress Playground: the plugin goes in the .zip and runs before the import.

  • Advanced Custom Fields to staging

    Recipes built with Advanced Custom Fields (ACF) — their post type, taxonomy, field group and values — taken to staging with every photo, page and term they point to pointing right.

  • Elementor to staging

    A landing page built with Elementor — its images, a saved section template and the site's global colors and fonts — taken to staging with Elementor's own importer, everything pointing to staging's items.
